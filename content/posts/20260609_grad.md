# Graduate

Hi again, long time no see. I graduated. It didn't feel very ceremonious and I didn't get a sense of finality from it all, but as it simmers and time goes on, I just feel really weird. I started my internship and the reality that the rest of my life is work is terrifying. I never thought I'd feel this way but I'm not ready to move on from school- thankfully, I have a year left. It'll be different, though.

I had a helluva semester and I couldn't be more thankful for all of my friends in Atlanta and Maryland. You guys have made the last 4 years of my life so memorable and it's hard to accept that everyone is moving so far away. Don't worry, I will be visiting.
