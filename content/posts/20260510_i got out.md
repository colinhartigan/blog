# I got out

There's some great stories of what Tech was like back in the day. One that's stuck with me is how at new student convocation, they'd tell the freshmen to look at the people to their right and to their left, and that statistically, only one out of three would graduate. Of course, Tech is very different now, but what a terrifying thing to hear as a freshman who just worked their ass off to get there in the first place.

I graduated this week.

Besides walking across stage, the whole ceremony and festivities didn't instill a sense of finality with the whole college thing. I guess that makes sense– I'll be back for one more year as a grad student, but still, a lot of my friends are leaving and a huge chapter of my life just closed.

Like anyone who's ever gone through college, towards the end there were parts of me that regretted not being more involved with certain activities, or not putting myself out to make more friends, but at the end of the day, it is what it is, and I'm certainly satisfied with the past four years of my life. The friends I have made are some of the most incredible people I've ever met. I am so proud of everyone, from study abroad friends, to Hive friends, to the urethra! crew, and to the UMD guys, I couldn't have gotten through it without y'all.
